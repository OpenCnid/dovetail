# Standing-model reframing (dated pointer — July 20, 2026)

Moved here from `SKILL.md` on 2026-08-01. It sat past the ~19,900-character
surviving prefix and was therefore absent from any compacted session that needed
it. Nothing was cut.

A ratified standing model reframes this protocol without changing its vocabulary
(`STANDING_MODEL.md`, principle only, no build; `RECONCILIATION.md` §7.2;
`JUDGE_COMPOSITION_GAME.md` §12) — all three mirrored in this directory:

- The verdict enum `clean | drawback | abstain` is the **sign of a signed delta**
  `+1 | −1 | 0` on one ternary standing axis (belief / doubt / fact) — a
  reframing of what the enum *is*, not an edit to the tokens or the mechanics.
- The panel **emits signed findings; the user gates whether standing moves**, in
  both directions. The disposing verbs in Step 7 (promote, merit-refuse) are
  therefore user acts the engine records, not panel acts — who holds the pen
  moved, the grammar did not.
- **Merit-refuse** is superseded in principle by **user-gated ratification**: a
  value-mode candidate the panel cannot dispute is recorded as user-gated, not
  refused into a silence indistinguishable from "we never looked."
- The six claim modes are **a useful first vocabulary, not a validated primitive
  partition** of assertion-space — lean on them as labels, not as a proven
  partition.
- A first-class **doubt / objection / defeater** tier exists
  (`DOUBTS_WORKSPACE.md`): a `−1` is constructed defeat with somewhere to live,
  not merely absent support.

These are ratified as principle and build nothing; the enum tokens and the Step-7
grammar stand until a separately gated build changes them.
