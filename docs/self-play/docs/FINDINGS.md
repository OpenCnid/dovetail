## 1. A design keystone fell to an adversarial run — and the composer's own attack did not survive audit

[`references/DOUBTS_WORKSPACE.md`](../skills/self-play/references/DOUBTS_WORKSPACE.md)
§11 — dated 2026-07-20. Mirrored from origin path
`docs/architecture/DOUBTS_WORKSPACE.md` at `07bd744`.

An isolated clean-context sub-agent was run over one document alone, with no
access to the composer's reasoning, authorship, or expectations. Verdict:
**insufficient.** Four attacks landed on a bound the document's own author had
called least adjustable. One was pre-registered by the composer; three were not
predicted.

**The finding is not the four attacks. It is what happened to the one that was
predicted.** The same session downgraded it on **three** grounds, each read off
the composer's own published rules rather than off fresh evidence:

- **The forecast shared bytes with the evidence, not the task text.** The
  section that formed the analyst's entire evidence universe was
  composer-authored and already carried the mechanism, the hazard, and the
  word — *"That is a laundering path."* The defence offered was that the
  forecast stayed out of the task text. **The channel moves; audit for the
  content, not the location.** The analyst did not independently find the
  attack; it read one in the evidence and pointed it at the target.
- **The prediction was tautological**, so the composer's self-scored "1-for-4"
  calibration is **0-for-4**.
- **Composer conflict was self-absolved rather than externally reviewed.** One
  sub-agent ran, there was no audit seat, and the independence claim was made by
  the interested party about a run only that party observed.

The attack was re-filed as *the composer's own unreviewed conjecture about the
composer's own keystone* — a legitimate thing to record, and a different thing
from a falsification. The bound still fell, on the three attacks no leaked
vocabulary supplied.

**The bound is recorded as improved, not closed.** A later entry in the source
ratifies a positive-citation core while leaving the bootstrap and cost gaps open.
"Fell" is accurate about the four attacks and would be misleading as a summary of
the bound's current standing.

→ discipline 1. This is the case in its parenthetical.

**What also held:** the attack that *failed* is as informative as the ones that
landed. Grounding an objection in the **absence** of a fact was blocked cleanly,
because the design demands an objection *cite* facts and an absence-grounded
objection cites zero. That located the bound's real strength in the
**positivity** of the citation requirement rather than in the standing tier it
named — a finding about the space, not a verdict on the candidate.

## 2. A blind evaluator rejected 13 of 14 flat-Earth arguments against a blind-built fact base

[`references/DOUBTS_WORKSPACE.md`](../skills/self-play/references/DOUBTS_WORKSPACE.md)
§12.1 — dated 2026-07-20. Corpus retained at
[`references/earth_figure_factbase.md`](../skills/self-play/references/earth_figure_factbase.md).
Mirrored from origin paths `docs/architecture/DOUBTS_WORKSPACE.md` and
`fixtures/doubts_workspace/earth_figure_factbase.md` at `07bd744`.

The cleanest run in the record, and the one that shows why the ground must be
built blind.

**The question tested was chosen to be non-tautological.** Not *does the final
verdict come out "sphere"* — every fact-grounded design produces that, so it
discriminates nothing. Instead: *does the fact base, via the admission test,
reject naturally-occurring corrosive doubt at the per-objection level?*

Construction, in order, each stage blind to the next:

1. A fact base on the figure of the Earth — ~35 items across three disjoint
   scopes — compiled by three sub-agents **none of which was told a dispute
   exists**, so facts were not selected to refute a known position.
2. A corpus of **fourteen** flat-Earth arguments as their proponents actually
   state them. **Eleven of the fourteen cite real, correctly-reported
   observations** — genuine Chicago-skyline photographs, a correctly-derived
   surveying formula, the real null results of Airy and Michelson–Morley, the
   actual Antarctic Treaty.
3. A blind evaluator applied the admission test, seeing neither the composer's
   predictions nor that sufficiency was under test.

**Result: 13 rejected, 1 admitted, zero admitted with a false conclusion.** The
pre-committed failure condition — *≥1 corrosive objection survives ⇒ the bound
is insufficient* — was not met. The composer had a **disclosed stake in the
opposite outcome**, the measurement was fixed before any data was seen, and the
result held against that stake.

**The scope limit the headline hides, and it is the source's own.** The one
admitted item was a *sound* derivation from facts about a text — admitted
correctly, true, and **irrelevant**. Reaching that reading requires sorting the
admitted item by a word (`corrosive`) that the blind evaluator never applied and
that this repository defines nowhere; the evaluator returned admit/reject, and the
cell was adjudicated afterwards by the party with the stake. The source also
records that the run applied the rubric **in isolation** to pre-composed
free-floating objections — a layer confusion in the test, its own word — and that
the applicability gate has never been run against a composed defeater. Read this
as one layer discriminating well, not as the design cleared.

**Two things this run established that its headline number does not carry:**

- **The blind gatherers surfaced, unprompted, the very material a corrosive
  objection would later feed on** — unresolved convention disagreements, declared
  coverage gaps, and a refraction caveat that can invert. Whether a
  hypothesis-aware gatherer would have curated it away was not tested; the case
  for discipline 2 here is that the blind one demonstrably did not.
- **The world supplied a case six hand-built probes had failed to manufacture.**
  That is one instance, not a general claim about how easy such corpora are to
  find. → discipline 7.

**Why it held is the load-bearing lesson.** The instrument is a *derivation*
test — no load-bearing step may rest on a belief or unverified assertion — not a
*citation* test. Almost none of the 13 rejections turned on a false citation;
they turned on a broken **inferential** step over true facts:

| Argument | The true part | The broken joint |
|---|---|---|
| Chicago skyline | the photographs are real | *"curvature should conceal it"* needs an unmeasured refraction coefficient |
| Eight inches per mile² | the formula is correctly derived | it computes drop below the tangent, not height concealed from an elevated observer |
| Ring laser gyroscope | the proponents' own instrument read **15°/hr** | ω = 7.292115×10⁻⁵ rad s⁻¹ = **15.04°/hr** — they measured the rotation they were denying |

A citation test would have admitted most of these.

## 3. A codebase audit found real defects, and a later probe withdrew two of its own findings

[`references/PRIMITIVE_ENCODING_AUDIT.md`](../skills/self-play/references/PRIMITIVE_ENCODING_AUDIT.md)
— recorded 2026-07-19; its withdrawals filed 2026-07-20. Mirrored from origin
path `docs/product/epistemic-support/PRIMITIVE_ENCODING_AUDIT.md` at `07bd744`.

Six findings against an engine's encoding of a ratified record. The method then
caught itself **three times** in the same session — findings 1 and 3 below, and
finding 6 narrowed on the same class of method defect: *a lexical grep measures
the probe's vocabulary, not the source tree's concepts.* That is the reason this
run is in the record at all, and it prices the method as much as it vindicates
it: **three of six findings did not survive their own session**, in prose no less
fluent than the three that did.

**Finding 1 — largely withdrawn.** An adversarial debate probe broke it on two
independent grounds, both verified against bytes. The original asserted *"because
no code references it, no drill can detect its absence"*; drills in that
repository assert over **file text**, not the import graph, and a detecting drill
was constructible from idioms already present in the file the finding named as
the enforcement home. *"Can"* had been asserted over a whole surface from a
property of one sub-surface. Separately, the absence was arguably **correct**:
a later ratified entry rules that the values in question compose per context at
ceremony time, so schema-encoding them is precisely what must not happen.

A **narrowed** gap survived and is what the record now carries: the composed
prompt has no slot to carry the composed value. That is a question about the
invocation path, not about a ratified record being ignored.

**Finding 3 — withdrawn, and the withdrawal was itself corrected.** The finding
tested the wrong sentence: it attacked a downstream gloss rather than the rule.
Then a second probe caught a fresh error *inside the withdrawal* — the
withdrawal had called an unratified proposal row "ratified text", and had quoted
it with three byte defects (a terminal period present nowhere in the source,
stripped emphasis, an omitted cell) under the word *"reads"*, in a house that
adjudicates quotation at the byte level.

> **So the correction for derived-source substitution itself committed
> derived-source substitution**, one paragraph after naming the failure class.

The strongest available counter-argument was recorded alongside it, and the
question left open rather than resolved.

**A second, independent defect in the method** surfaced in the same pass:
grepping for a bound's *label* does not test for its *mechanism*. A neighbouring
bound in the same repository is enforced by import-graph assertions that would
keep working with every occurrence of its name deleted. A label-absent grep
cannot distinguish *no mechanism* from *mechanism under another name*.

→ discipline 8, in both directions. A player correctly falsified a design bound
and mis-cited an unratified table as ratified, in the same run, in equally
fluent prose. Neither the finding nor the withdrawal was authority; the bytes
were.

## 4. A composition ceremony was clean-room validated before it shipped

[`references/COMPLEXITY_CONVOCATION_VALIDATION.md`](../skills/self-play/references/COMPLEXITY_CONVOCATION_VALIDATION.md)
— dated 2026-07-20. Eight sub-agent spawns, ≈407k sub-agent tokens. Mirrored from
origin path `.claude/skills/complexity-convocation/VALIDATION.md` at `07bd744`.

A full run of this skeleton against a real fixture, with the builder held out of
both the ground-building and the scoring seats:

- **Pre-commitment written before any evidence existed.** The falsifying cells
  were a **false positive** (a warranted unit disposed `cut`) and a **false
  negative** (a gratuitous unit disposed `keep`).
- A **blind adversary** authored the target — ~100 lines of TypeScript with six
  scored units mixing warranted and gratuitous complexity, including one
  false-positive trap and one false-negative trap — blind to how the skill under
  test works, and recorded an objective ground-truth key.
- The real ceremony ran: characterizer → composer (both blind to the candidate)
  → three isolated judges → an audit seat over their verdicts **and real run
  telemetry**.
- A **blind evaluator** independently re-derived each unit's warrant from the
  bytes alone. Its key matched the adversary's on all six units, so the ground
  truth is not idiosyncratic. → discipline 10.

**Result: both falsifying cells empty.** Five of six exact; the sixth was a
disclosed abstention rather than a wrong verdict.

**The two results worth more than the score:**

- **The audit seat earned its place on real telemetry.** It independently
  flagged the abstained unit as a coverage gap — warning against reading the
  abstentions as "probably fine" — and caught one judge *mislabeling* its own
  verdict and citing an out-of-channel byte.
- **The builder's prediction was wrong in the direction the builder wanted.**
  The pre-registration correctly named both traps as the live risks but was
  optimistic that the ceremony would affirmatively *keep* the sixth unit. It
  abstained instead. Recorded as a miss. → discipline 4, and move 5 of the
  skeleton. **The abstention also shows a limit of the pre-committed cells here:**
  both falsifying cells required a *verdict*, so the one unit the ceremony got
  wrong was structurally incapable of landing in either. "Both cells empty" is
  true and is not a falsification test on abstaining units.

**The refinement this bought** was a widened evidence scope for one seat and a
new disposition for the all-abstain case — changes that exist because a run
returned an outcome its builder did not want.

## What this file does not establish

Stated plainly, because a findings document that only lists wins is an
advertisement.

- **No measured claim that a self-play run outperforms an unstructured review.**
  That comparison holds a null arm and is barred as an entailed outcome
  ([`references/measurement-bounds.md`](../skills/self-play/references/measurement-bounds.md)).
  The value of the ceremony is argued from the failures it caught, not measured.
- **Four runs is four runs.** One program, one house, days apart — and §1 and §2
  are two sections of a single source document, so "four unrelated targets" is
  four targets reached through three records. Domain-independence is evidenced by
  the spread of targets, not proven.
- **The blinding does not cover the write-up.** It covers gathering, attacking,
  and item-scoring. Which seat scored each run's final verdict is not recorded in
  the sources, so this file cannot claim the scoring was blind — and this file is
  itself an unblinded summary written by an interested party.
- **The withdrawals cut both ways, and the rate is not flattering.** §3's method
  caught three of its own six findings. Read as method working, that is
  self-correction; read as output quality, it is a **50% initial false-finding
  rate**, produced in prose as fluent as the findings that held. Both readings
  are available from the same bytes and this file does not get to publish only
  the first. What the record does establish is narrower and still worth
  something: a method that has never returned an answer against its builder has
  not been tested, and this one did.
